About the Pup

This is my hound Martta, my loyal companion and favorite outdoor adventure buddy. Whether we are walking in the forest, relaxing on the couch, or playing fetch, there is never a boring day with her around.

Taking care of a pet teaches patience, consistency, and the joy of simple things. This page is dedicated to celebrating our daily chaos and favorite moments together.
